# Beginner to Intermediate General Fitness Weekly Workout Plan

# Weekly Workout Plan for Andreea (Beginner, Goal: Build Muscle)

**Overview:** This plan balances strength training, cardio, and flexibility to improve overall fitness while supporting muscle growth. Workouts assume access to dumbbells, barbells, bench, cable machine, and bodyweight options. Adjust weights to allow completion of prescribed reps with good form; increase gradually as you get stronger.

---

## Monday – Full‑Body Strength
| Exercise | Sets | Reps / Duration | Notes |
|----------|------|----------------|-------|
| Goblet Squat (dumbbell) | 3 | 12‑15 | Keep chest up, sit back into heels |
| Push‑Up (or Bench Press with light bar) | 3 | 10‑12 (push‑up) or 8‑10 (bench) | Full range, elbows ~45° |
| Bent‑Over Dumbbell Row | 3 | 12‑15 each side | Slight knee bend, flat back |
| Dumbbell Shoulder Press | 3 | 10‑12 | Avoid arching lower back |
| Plank | 3 | 30‑45 sec | Engage core, neutral spine |
| Rest between sets | – | 60‑90 sec | – |

*Focus on mastering form; use a weight that feels challenging by the last 2‑3 reps.*

---
